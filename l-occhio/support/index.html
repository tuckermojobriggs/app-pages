l'occhio Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email tuckermojo@gmail.com and tell us which iPhone and which version of l'occhio you have (Settings › General › About shows the iPhone model; the App Store page shows the app's version). A person reads every message.

Common questions

A crew or plan isn't showing up on another phone. Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud and online. Open l'occhio on both; changes usually arrive within a minute.

I joined a crew, but it's empty. Joined crews take a moment to load after the app opens. If the Crews tab says it's still syncing, wait, or tap Retry.

Plans aren't in Apple Calendar. Check Settings › Apple Calendar in l'occhio: "Mirror to Apple Calendar" must be on and calendar access allowed. Only plans you're Going to are mirrored, into a calendar named "l'occhio".

How do I delete my data? Settings › Delete my data. The privacy policy explains exactly what it removes and what it can't.

Someone in a crew is bothering me. Open the crew, tap Manage, and choose Report or Block next to their name. You can also report a single plan from the plan itself. Blocking takes effect straight away on your phone; reports come to us by email. We reply to every report within 24 hours. We can't see or remove anything ourselves, because your crews live in iCloud, not with us. We'll help you block the person (Manage › Block), leave the crew (Manage › Leave crew), or, if the crew is yours, remove them from it (Manage › Remove someone…).

I deleted a plan by mistake. Settings › Recently Deleted keeps deleted plans and crews for 30 days.
