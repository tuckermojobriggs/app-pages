l'occhio Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

l'occhio is a calendar for small groups of friends, called crews. We built it so that we never hold your data. We have no server.

Where your plans live

Your crews, plans, photos and RSVPs are stored in your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. A crew you create lives in your iCloud. When you invite people, Apple's CloudKit sharing lets the members of that crew see and edit it. A crew you join lives in the iCloud account of whoever created it.

Your plans are shared only with the members of the crews you post them to. There are no public profiles, and nobody can find you or see when you're free. We, the developer, cannot see anyone's data.

Apple Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Mirror to Apple Calendar" and give permission, l'occhio copies the plans you're going to into a calendar called "l'occhio" on your iPhone. That copy is handled by Apple's Calendar app, and it syncs only through your own calendar account if you use one. Turning the switch off removes the entries l'occhio wrote. It never touches events you added yourself.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, the app receives only a stable id that Apple creates for this app, and your name, which fills in the name shown on your RSVPs (you can change it in Settings). We don't ask Apple for your email address. The id is stored on your iPhone and marks which RSVPs are yours, so you count as one person across your Apple devices.

Reminders

Reminders for plans you're going to (a day before and an hour before) are local notifications. They are scheduled on your iPhone and are not sent through any server.

Photos

l'occhio reads your photo library only with your permission, and only copies the photo you pick into a plan. That photo syncs to the crew the same way the plan does.

What we don't do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party servers or code that collect data. We don't sell or share any data, because we don't have any.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Delete my data deletes the crews you created (for everyone in them, since they live in your iCloud) and every RSVP you've written. It can also take you out of the crews you joined.
  • Leaving a crew (Crews › the crew › Leave crew) removes it from your phone. It does not touch the owner's copy.
  • Plans you added to someone else's crew live in that person's iCloud. Delete them from the plan first, or ask the crew's owner to.
  • To remove everything l'occhio stored in your iCloud, open the Settings app and go to your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) › l'occhio. Deleting the app also removes the copy on your iPhone.

Reporting and blocking

In a crew's Manage screen, or on a plan, you can report or block a person, and you can report a single plan.

  • Blocking hides that person's plans, RSVPs and name in your app straight away. It only affects your phone and nothing is deleted. Unblock any time in Settings › Blocked people.
  • Reporting opens an email to us in your own Mail app, so you can see exactly what is sent and cancel. It contains the reason you picked, the plan's or person's name, and the ids we need to identify them. No photo is attached. A person reads every report. We reply to every report within 24 hours. We can't see or remove anything ourselves, because your crews live in iCloud, not with us. We'll help you block the person (Manage › Block), leave the crew (Manage › Leave crew), or, if the crew is yours, remove them from it (Manage › Remove someone…).
