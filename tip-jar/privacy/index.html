Tip Jar — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Tip Jar does not collect your data. There is no tracking, and no server belonging to us. What you put into the app stays on your iPhone and, if you are signed into iCloud, in your own iCloud account — which we cannot see into.

What Tip Jar stores, and where

WhatWhere it is kept
Bills, tips, splits, dates and notesOn your iPhone, and in your own iCloud
Photographs of receiptsThe same, deleted when you delete the tip
The place a tip was left, if you add oneThe same
Your presetsThe same
Your settingsOn your iPhone
Your name, if you sign in with AppleOn your iPhone

Deleting a saved tip deletes its photograph and its location with it. “Delete all data” in Settings removes everything at once, from your iPhone and from iCloud.

iCloud

If you are signed into iCloud, Tip Jar keeps a copy of your tips and presets in your own private iCloud area, so your other iPhones and iPads show the same history. That area belongs to your Apple Account, not to us: we have no way to read it, and no copy of it ever reaches us. Apple looks after it under its own privacy policy.

There is nothing to switch on. If you are signed out of iCloud, Tip Jar keeps everything on your iPhone instead and works exactly the same. Settings tells you which of the two is happening.

Signing in with Apple

This is optional and it does very little. It puts your name on the Settings screen. That is all it is for.

Tip Jar asks Apple for your name only — never your email address. Apple gives back your name and an identifier that is unique to Tip Jar and useless anywhere else. Both are kept on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send them.

You can undo it at any time. Settings → Account → Delete account signs you out and deletes every tip, preset and setting, from your iPhone and from iCloud. You can also remove Tip Jar from your Apple Account in the iPhone's own Settings, under your name; Tip Jar notices and signs you out.

The one thing that goes to somebody else

If you choose to note where you left a tip, Tip Jar turns the coordinates into a place name — “Apple Park, Cupertino”, say. Producing that name asks Apple Maps, so the coordinates go to Apple, in the same way they would in any app that shows you a map. Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy. Tip Jar receives only the name back, and keeps it on your iPhone.

Nothing else is sent to anyone. If you never press “Add where I am”, no location is ever read or sent.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph a receipt so the total can be read. Asked for when you press “Scan a receipt”.
  • Location, while using the app — only to note where a tip was left. Asked for when you press “Add where I am”, never at launch and never in the background.
  • Notifications — only for the optional daily reminder. Asked for when you turn that switch on.

Refusing any of these leaves the rest of the app working normally.

Purchases

Tip Jar Pro and the optional tips for the developer are handled entirely by Apple. Tip Jar never sees your payment details, and keeps no record of what you bought — it asks Apple what you own each time it starts.

Tracking and analytics

There are none. Tip Jar contains no advertising identifier, no analytics, and no third-party software of any kind.

Children

Tip Jar is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Tip Jar ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version is released.
