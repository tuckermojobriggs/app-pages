Tip Jar — Support

Tip Jar works out a tip, splits a bill so every cent is accounted for, and keeps a history on your iPhone.

Common questions

The split does not divide evenly. Who pays the extra?
The first person. Tip Jar says so underneath the results, so the shares always add back up to the total and no cents go missing.
Scanning a receipt says there is no camera.
You are probably on a device without one, such as the Simulator. Use “Choose a photo” instead.
It read the wrong amount off my receipt.
Tip Jar shows you what it found before using it, so you can correct it on that screen. It looks for a line naming a total; if none exists it takes the largest amount and tells you it has done so.
I bought Tip Jar Pro and it is not unlocked on my new iPhone.
Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore button on the Pro screen. Tip Jar keeps no record of its own; it asks Apple what your account owns.
Does a tip to the developer unlock anything?
No. It is only a thank-you. Everything Pro unlocks comes with Pro.
Where is my data?
On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud account if you are signed into iCloud. There is no server of ours involved, and we cannot see any of it. See the privacy policy.
My tips are not showing up on my other iPhone.
Both need to be signed into the same iCloud account. Open Settings in Tip Jar and look at the iCloud line: it says in plain words whether it is up to date, waiting, or signed out.
Do I have to sign in with Apple?
No. It is optional and unlocks nothing — it only puts your name on the Settings screen. Tip Jar asks for your name and nothing else, not even an email address.
How do I delete my account?
Settings → Account → Delete account. It signs you out and removes every tip, preset and setting, from your iPhone and from iCloud.
How do I get my history out?
History tab → the share button. It exports a CSV that opens in Numbers, Excel or anything else that reads a spreadsheet.
