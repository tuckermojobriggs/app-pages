Miss You Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Miss You does not collect any data about you. Nothing you enter or do in the app is sent to the developer.

Miss You contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What other people can see

Miss You lets you share with other people. What you share (your approximate current location, so your partner can see how far away you are) can be seen by the one person you pair with through an iCloud invite link. None of it is sent to the developer.

To report something another person shared with you, write to Tuckermojo@gmail.com.

Signing in

Miss You has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data from inside the app: the people list (tap Stop pairing) and Stop sharing on the Distance screen.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Tuckermojo@gmail.com.
