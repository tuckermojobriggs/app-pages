Gate of Bones Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Gate of Bones does not collect any data about you. Nothing you enter or do in the app is sent to Tucker Briggs or shared with anyone else. What you create in the app stays on your device, or in your own Apple account if you use iCloud.

Gate of Bones contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
