Gate of Bones Support

Questions, problems or ideas about Gate of Bones? Email tuckermojo@gmail.com and say which iPhone and which version of the app you have. Every message is read.

Report a challenge

If a friend sends you a challenge with a message that is offensive or unwanted, open Challenges, tap … on that challenge and choose Report challenge to hide it, or Block sender to stop seeing their challenges. Then email tuckermojo@gmail.com with the sender's name and the message, and it will be looked at promptly.

Deleting your data

Settings › Privacy › Delete my data removes your progress, challenges, castle banner, reminders and the challenge night calendar from your iPhone and your iCloud.
