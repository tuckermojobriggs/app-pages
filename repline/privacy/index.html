Repline: Workout Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Repline: Workout Tracker does not collect any data about you. Nothing you enter or do in the app is sent to the developer or shared with anyone else. What you create in the app stays on your device, or in your own Apple account if you use iCloud.

Repline: Workout Tracker contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Signing in

Repline: Workout Tracker has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Other parts of your iPhone

Repline: Workout Tracker works with:

  • Apple Health: writes each finished workout to Apple Health, only if you allow it; Repline never reads anything from Health

Where iOS asks for your permission first, you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security. Nothing the app reads or writes there is sent to the developer.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data from inside the app: Settings › Erase All Workout Data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
